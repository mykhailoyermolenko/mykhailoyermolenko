# Mykhailo Yermolenko

Design Engineer in Tallinn, Estonia: websites, startup launches, and brand systems.

I design in Figma and ship the production code myself in Astro, React, and TypeScript. Over the last few years I have taken around 15 client projects from early positioning and visual identity through to live, accessible websites. Working as one person across design and frontend means the typography, spacing, and motion in the Figma file actually survive into the browser.

At [Soily.World](https://mykhailo.site/work/soily/) (2024 to 2026), I led the brand identity, website build, and investor pitch materials that helped get the team into the Tehnopol Startup Incubator, into the sTARTUp Day Top 25, and through 50 to 100 investor and partner pitches. Before moving to Estonia, I built and ran the design function at [TURBO ZNO](https://mykhailo.site/work/turbo-zno/) (2022 to 2024), creating the visual system, course-enrolment site, and template library that supported around 12 simultaneous product launches as the school grew to seven-figure turnover.

I work in Figma and the Adobe suite on the design side, and in Astro, React, TypeScript, and GSAP on the code side, building to WCAG 2.2 AA accessibility (EAA-ready) and Core Web Vitals budgets. I speak English, Estonian, Ukrainian, and Russian, and I am open to Design Engineer or Web & Product Designer roles as well as B2B client projects.
